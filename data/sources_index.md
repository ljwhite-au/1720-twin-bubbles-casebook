# Primary-Source Bibliography & Archival Transparency Log (`data/sources_index.md`)

**The 1720 Twin Bubbles: John Law's *Système* & The South Sea Bubble**

Every quantitative figure in `data/*.csv`, `workbook/*.xlsx`, `casebook.md`, and `1720_Twin_Bubbles_Casebook.pdf` derives from pre-1923 public-domain primary sources.

## Part I — Paris (1716–1721) Primary Sources
1. **Sir James Steuart (1767),** *An Inquiry into the Principles of Political Oeconomy*, Vol. 2, Book IV, Part II (*Banque Générale* 6M capital, 1717–1719 share tranches, 624,000 total shares, explicit 1719 note *arrêts*, 22 Feb 1720 merger, 1.3B coin estimate).
2. **Edmond-Jean-François Barbier (1718–1763; 1857 ed.),** *Chronique de la Régence et du règne de Louis XV (Journal de Barbier)*, Mars–Décembre 1720 (eyewitness Paris records of the 21/27 May *arrêts*, Place Vendôme / Hôtel de Soissons trading, July 1720 vault-count refusal by Treasurer Bourgeois to Fagon & Trudaine, and 10 Oct 1720 banknote demonetization).
3. **John Philip Wood (1824),** *Memoirs of the Life of John Law of Lauriston* (511,009 *Commission du Visa* filings; 2,222,597,491 *livres* gross claims).
4. **Charles Moran (1863),** *Money* (48,000,000 *livres*/year post-*Visa* Crown interest commitment).
5. **William Graham Sumner / Pierre des Essars (1896),** *A History of Banking in All the Leading Nations*, Vol. 3 (citing André Cochut: 2,696,400,000 *livres* gross notes; 707,327,460 *livres* retired).
6. **Charles A. Conant (1915),** *A History of Modern Banks of Issue* & ***1911 Encyclopædia Britannica*** ("Law, John").

## Part II — London (1711–1723) Primary Sources
1. **Statutes of the Realm:** *9 Anne c. 21* (1711 Charter), *6 Geo. I c. 4* (1720 South Sea Act), *6 Geo. I c. 18* (1720 Bubble Act), *7 Geo. I c. 28* (1721 South Sea Sufferers Act), *9 Geo. I c. 6* (1723 4% Annuities).
2. ***History and Proceedings of the House of Commons*, Vol. 6 (pp. 218–262):** Dec 1720–Jan 1721 Secret Committee inquiry, 259–117 contract vote, Sunderland 233–172 acquittal, Aislabie & Craggs Sr. proceedings.
3. **John Noorthouck (1773),** *A New History of London* (pp. 306–325: 19 May 1720 conversion terms, Four Money Subscriptions, 20 Sept 400% reduction, complete 33-estate statutory schedule).
4. **John Francis (1847),** *History of the Bank of England*; **Walter Thornbury (1878),** *Old and New London* (Pool A £574,500 Green Book itemized lines & Caswall £250k refund); **Charles Mackay (1841),** *Extraordinary Popular Delusions* (Pool B £936k allocations); **Sir John Blunt (1722),** *True State of the South-Sea-Scheme* (Surman £112,321 transposition); **Adam Smith (1776),** *Wealth of Nations*, Book V, Ch. 3; **Eugen von Philippovich (1911)** & **Norris Brisco (1907)**.
